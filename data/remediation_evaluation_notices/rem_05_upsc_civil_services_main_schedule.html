UNION PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

Notice: Timetable for Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026

F.1/8/2026-E.I(B)

Union Public Service Commission will conduct the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026 commencing from 22nd January 2027.

The Examination will be held on 22nd, 23rd, 24th, 29th and 30th January 2027 in two sessions (9:00 AM to 12:00 Noon and 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM).

The e-Admit Cards for the candidates will be uploaded on the Commission's website 3 weeks before the date of commencement of the examination.
