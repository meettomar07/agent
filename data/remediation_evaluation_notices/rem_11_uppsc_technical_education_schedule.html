UTTAR PRADESH PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

Notice No. 58/E-3/2026

Notice regarding Examination Schedule for Technical Education (Teaching) Service Examination 2026.

The Examination will be held on 14th March 2027 in two shifts (9:30 AM to 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM).

Candidates can download their Admit Card from the Commission's website 10 days before the exam date.
